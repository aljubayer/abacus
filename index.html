How to practise

  • Add each column from top to bottom. A minus sign means take that number away.
  • Do a level on the abacus first, then do it again in your head by picturing the beads.
  • Get every answer right before you go for speed. Real contests are quick: ALOHA Bangladesh’s national round is 70 sums in 5 minutes.
  • Press Check answers to mark your work, fix the red ones, and check again.
