Abacus mind math · Level 2

Speed practice: 200 questions

Addition and subtraction from basic to advanced, using small friends (5) and big friends (10). No combination (mix-friend) formulas.

How to use

  1. Numbers with no sign are added. Numbers with − are taken away.
  2. Solve each section on the abacus first, then again as mind math.
  3. Press Show beside a question to check the answer.

Friends to remember
